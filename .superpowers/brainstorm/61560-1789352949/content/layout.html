L1 · Stacked

Status cards, buttons, log stacked top-to-bottom. Log gets whatever height is left. Simple, single reading column, works at any width. Log grows as it fills.

L2 · Split — docked log

Controls left, log docked right as a fixed dark panel filling full height. Log always visible, never pushed off. Best on wide monitors — the typical gate-box screen.

L3 · Hero status

One big system banner dominates: green "OPERATIONAL" or red "DEGRADED" with the cause inline and START right there. Glance-from-across-the-room. Cards become small chips.

Click one. L2 assumes a wide gate-box monitor; L1 is safest at any size; L3 optimizes for reading state from a distance.
